FAQ

Common questions.

Kya yeh real Amazon data use karta hai? +
Pro aur Agency plans mein Amazon SP-API se real data aata hai. Starter plan mein demo data hota hai. Hum sirf read karte hain — koi bhi action (ad pause, review reply) aapki approval ke baad hota hai.
Payment kaise hogi? UPI/card accept hota hai? +
Razorpay pe payment hogi — UPI, credit card, debit card, net banking sab accept hota hai. Har mahine automatic subscription cut hogi. Cancel karna ho to ek click mein band.
Kya free trial mein card details deni hogi? +
Nahi. 7-day free trial ke liye sirf email aur naam chahiye. Trial khatam hone pe aap decide karo — koi automatic charge nahi.
AI agent kya galat action le sakta hai? +
Nahi. Agent sirf read karta hai aur suggestions deta hai. Koi bhi action — ad pause, review reply, listing edit — pehle aapko approve karna hota hai. Autonomous posting nahi hoti.
Agency plan mein white-label kya hota hai? +
Aap apne clients ko apni branding ke saath dashboard de sakte ho. "SellerPilot" ki jagah aapka logo aur naam dikhega. Clients ko lagega yeh aapka khud ka tool hai.
